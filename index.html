Frequently Asked Questions

What does CareGap do?

CareGap builds healthcare infrastructure that companies deliver under their own brand. LabLink connects your product to Quest Diagnostics for lab ordering and results. EasyDonor runs living-donor programs for tissue banks and biobanks. The CareGap build team can also design and build a branded app or portal on top of them.

How do I start with LabLink?

Create an account at lablink.caregap.io. Your sandbox opens right away, before any payment, so you can build against the API with synthetic data. Going live takes a signed Platform Agreement and BAA, onboarding, a verified ordering provider and a Quest subaccount. See the first sandbox order walkthrough.

Can everything be branded to my company?

Yes. LabLink runs behind your own app, so intake, results and patient communication carry your brand. Each EasyDonor program is built around the bank's own criteria, forms and brand.

How is pricing set?

Each product has its own pricing. LabLink pricing is published in full on the LabLink page. EasyDonor programs and custom builds are scoped and priced to your goals. LabLink pricing.

Can it integrate with my systems?

LabLink is a REST API with separate sandbox and production environments and signed webhooks, documented publicly with an OpenAPI spec. For EasyDonor or a custom build, we scope the integrations with you. LabLink API docs.

How is patient data handled?

On HIPAA-compliant infrastructure, under a BAA. Patient data is retained under the organization that created it, and CareGap never sells it.

Ready to build?

Developers can start in the LabLink sandbox today. For EasyDonor, a custom build, or help scoping LabLink, book a time with us.
